The big idea

Everything lands in one book

The till is a friendly front end on a strict accounting engine. When the cashier completes a sale, the system records the money in, the stock out and its cost, and any loyalty points earned, all in one step. Reports read that ledger directly, so the P&L, Balance Sheet and stock value always agree with each other. This is the engine proven in Zeno, reused here.

What that means for the owner

  • No separate accounting package, and no end-of-month data entry
  • Profit, cash and stock value are always current
  • Mistakes get corrected with a reversal, never deleted, so there's a full trail

Stock in

Supplier VAT stays in the cost

Kenfri isn't VAT registered, so it can't claim back the VAT it pays suppliers; the full VAT-inclusive amount is what stock costs. Lush prints VAT-inclusive rates: the lines add up to 6,960 and the 960 VAT is backed out of that total, so the leave-in costs exactly KES 25.00. Other suppliers add VAT on top of a net rate, so every bill has a "rates include VAT" switch.

Receiving a supplier invoice

  • Each product remembers the supplier's item code and carton size, so typing FGWHDLIT01-40 finds it
  • Stock is costed first-in, first-out (FIFO), so margins are accurate
  • What we owe the supplier is tracked until it's paid
  • Barcodes: scan the manufacturer's, or generate an in-store one if there isn't one
  • Weekly stock take by scanning; the owner approves differences
Customers

Loyalty, credit and knowing who buys what

Loyalty points (modelled on Carrefour MyCLUB)

  • 1 point = KES 0.10; earn rate to be confirmed (draft: 1 point per KES 10 spent)
  • Points can be redeemed once they're worth KES 100 (1,000 points)
  • Wholesale customers don't earn points unless the owner switches it on for them
  • Points are recorded as money owed to customers until they're spent

Credit accounts

  • Available to salons, wholesale and retail customers, each with a credit limit
  • Statements, aging and SMS reminders
  • The system suggests limits from each customer's buying history; the owner approves them (v1.5)

Returns

24 hours, unopened only

Rules the till enforces

  • Returns only with the original receipt
  • Within 24 hours of the sale; after that, the owner's PIN is needed
  • The cashier confirms each item is unopened
  • Exchange is the default. Cash refunds need the owner's PIN
  • Returned stock goes back on the shelf; points from the sale are taken back

StaffReceive stock, stock take, add products, suggest prices.

Not in v1
